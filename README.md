- **Drag and Drop Technology** — Simplify your workflow with intuitive drag and drop capabilities.
- **Freeze Function** — Optimize your system's performance by freezing tracks for efficient resource management.
- **Automatic Delay Compensation** — Ensure perfect timing in your mixes with automatic delay adjustments.
